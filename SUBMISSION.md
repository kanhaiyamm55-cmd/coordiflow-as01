# AS-01 Submission Draft

## Project
CoordiFlow — Coordination Intelligence System

## The idea
CoordiFlow solves a specific coordination failure in architecture and construction projects: when one stakeholder changes something, the team often lacks a shared view of who is affected and what must happen next.

Instead of building another task manager, CoordiFlow creates an impact-aware coordination layer.

## Demo story
1. Open the Modern Villa project.
2. Show stakeholders and their responsibilities.
3. Record: “Client wants to move the kitchen sink to the opposite wall.”
4. Show the intelligence result: likely plumbing, electrical, cabinet and kitchen-layout impacts.
5. Show the affected stakeholders.
6. Show automatically created review actions.
7. Mark an action complete.
8. Open the activity/project-memory view and show the change and resolution history.

## Why this is useful
The value is not another place to store tasks. The value is connecting a change to its consequences and responsible people, reducing missed handoffs and hidden dependencies.

## 3–5 minute video structure
0:00–0:30 — Problem and scenario
0:30–1:00 — Stakeholder map
1:00–2:15 — Create a change and show impact identification
2:15–3:00 — Show generated actions and complete one
3:00–3:40 — Show project memory and alerts
3:40–4:30 — Architecture + intelligence approach + future scope

## One-line pitch
“When something changes, CoordiFlow tells the project who is affected, what may be blocked, and what needs to happen next.”
